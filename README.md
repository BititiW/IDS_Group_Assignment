# Measuring Progress Towards the UN Sustainable Development Goal 8: Decent Work and Economic Growth

## Overview
In 2015, The United Nations adopted the 2030
Agenda for Sustainable Development and its 17
Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), aiming to
end poverty, protect the planet and tackle inequalities.
In this report, we have assessed the progress of six
continents – Africa, Asia, Europe, North America,
South America, and Oceania – towards meeting SDG
8: Decent Work and Economic Growth. In particular,
we performed exploratory data analysis to assess
how these continents are faring with respect to Target
8.1 and 8.6.

### Part 1
Analysing whether regions meet Target 8.1: Sustain per capita economic growth in accordance with national circumstances and, in particular, at least 7 per cent gross domestic product growth per annum in the least developed countries.

### Part 2
Analysis whether regions meet Target 8.6: By 2020, substantially reduce the proportion of youth not in employment, education or training.

## General findings
Overall, our findings have shown that most
continents did not reduce NEET between 2015 and
2020. Improvements where present were small or
reversed — especially in 2020. These patterns
suggest that pre-existing progress in reducing youth
NEET rates is highly sensitive to external conditions
and heavily dependent on stable economic
environments. Reducing NEET on a sustained basis
therefore requires long-term structural reforms, as
well as greater resilience in labour markets and
education systems to withstand shocks like the
pandemic.
